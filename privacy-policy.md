# Privacy Policy – Ruyaai
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-10

## 1. Introduction
Ruyaai (“we”, “our”, “Ruyaai”) is an online educational platform that allows users to access paid video lessons and educational courses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Email, password, and user ID for login and authentication.
- **Subscription Information:** Active subscription status through RevenueCat, Google Play, and Apple IAP.
- **App Usage Data:** Basic app interactions and usage patterns to improve the App’s functionality.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To authenticate your account and grant access to paid content.
- To manage subscriptions and entitlement access.
- To maintain, improve, and optimize the App experience.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- [Google Play Billing](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Apple IAP](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 5. Payments & Subscription
All payments for premium content are handled securely by third-party services. We do not store your credit/debit card information directly.

## 6. Data Security
- Data is stored securely.
- Sensitive information such as secret keys is never stored in the app.
- All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 7. User Rights
- You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at **ruyaaiorg@gmail.com**.
- You can manage your subscription directly through Google Play or Apple IAP.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in the App and take effect immediately.

## 9. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at **ruyaaiorg@gmail.com**.
